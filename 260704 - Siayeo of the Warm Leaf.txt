**Siayeo of the Warm Leaf : Flesh Divinity**

--

The Warm Leaf is one of the more unusual, yet fundamental teachings of the Siayeo, originating among the pilgrims of Medijan in the 5th century. Their beliefs created the Arks, through which the Great Tree first reached across the sea, and contributed to the origin of the Lotus Sects and their brass effigies. All are rooted in the creed of corporeal continuity: that it matters not where and when the divine does flow, only that it does not still. According to the dogma of the Warm Leaf , living beings, with their beating hearts, are blessed from the moment they partake of the sacred waters - and with enough effort, that blessing can be ingrained deeply into one's being.

Vessels of the Warm Leaf undergo numerous rites to offer their blood and spirit to the Great Tree, using meditation, prometheum treatments, and a wide array of bodily and thaumaturgical techniques to live solely by recirculation once parted from the sacred waters. Such practices take their toll upon the body, and can easily cause deadly illness if improperly conducted. In return, Vessels gain the ability to commune and carry the sacred flow unassisted, growing naturally in attunement through habituation to the thaumaturgic essences; they can wield the sacred waters and even their own bodies in the fashion of the Great Tree, to such a point that elderly Vessels often begin dissociating from their physical forms. Among practitioners of the Warm Leaf, it is believed that brass effigies are inhabited with greater frequency by the remnants of Vessels who have finally escaped their corporeal existences.

It is a common mistaken belief that the Vessels' skills rely on basic hydrokinesis, which itself is only the most visible of the brass effigies' influences. To do so would require strongly joined flow between the waters beyond and the waters within, or rapid exsanguination. Instead, it is more accurate to say that most of the Warm Root's techniques rely on learning the temple script through the pulse of the heartbeat, which extends through the skin - and spreading awareness or influence both inwards through blood and outwards through sacred waters of the Great Tree, itself a metaphysical blood according to scriptures of the Warm Leaf. Vessels of the Warm Leaf have always been valued as medical practitioners for this reason, being able to diagnose and treat many illnesses adjacent to the blood, particularly of the heart and lungs; similarly, the fine manipulation they learn is well-suited to the work of chemists, geologists, and temple adepts.

Most Vessels of the Warm Leaf are able to suppress internal and external bleeding, treat the symptoms of cardiac arrest, avoid seasickness, still muscle tremors, and spontaneously rupture their own blood vessels, the latter practice being performed by Vessels looking to enhance their own appearance through careful control of ink and bruises. More practically, their increased compatibility with thaumic essences grants them the ability to seamlessly operate semi-open hydraulic machinery carrying the sacred waters of the Great Tree, something used to great effect in canalised workyards and coastal works.
